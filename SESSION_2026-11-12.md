## 2. Section B — equivalent formulations (`docs/LITERATURE.md` §B)

- **Proposition L [PROVED].** μ(G) ≥ λ(L(G)) = |L| − 2·min_{m meet-irreducible} |↓m|, with equality when every y_e (the union of the members avoiding e) is meet-irreducible.
- [OBSERVED, k = 3..5] μ < λ never occurs. μ > λ occurs in 1/2, 7/29 and 173/2,741 classes. **min λ = 0 at every k**, because valid families can have a Boolean lattice (example: {∅, 02, 12, 012}, μ = 2). So min μ(k) is a representation invariant, not a lattice invariant.
- Abe's excess ex(L) (abstract only) is ≥ 0 trivially. It differs from μ in 1/2, 15/29 and 1,946/2,741 classes, and its minimum is 0 [OBSERVED].
- The intersection-closed dual is a relabeling. "Frankl deficiency", "abundance gap" and "surplus" are not defined objects in any source read. Tian's deficit δ_a is a margin used only inside a counterexample argument.
- M_k: its ingredient (cloning points plus separating co-singletons) is Hu's proof device; the family and its μ were not found. B(…) and H_t were not found. Naming clash: Poonen's "block" and Morris's B(n,3,r).
