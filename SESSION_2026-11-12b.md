# Session 2026-11-12 (b) — ledger

Phase 8: an attempt to prove lower bounds on the margin μ. This is the second ledger dated 2026-11-12; `SESSION_2026-11-12.md` is the literature session. This session changed no existing file except `README.md` (one added paragraph). It added `docs/PROOF_SURVEY.md`, `docs/PROOF_ATTEMPT.md`, eight scripts under `proof/` and seven output files `results/proof_*.txt`. Tags are as in earlier ledgers: **[PROVED]** a hand proof, **[PROVED-EXH]** exhaustive computation with a certificate in `results/`, **[OBSERVED]** checked only at the stated k, **[FALSIFIED]** killed by a computed counterexample, plus **[CONJECTURE]** for unproved claims. None of this is peer-reviewed, and nothing here proves or verifies Frankl's conjecture.

Full statements, proofs, the novelty-check log and the delegation log are in `docs/PROOF_ATTEMPT.md`. This ledger is the index.

## 1. Section A — survey of proof techniques (`docs/PROOF_SURVEY.md`)

- Covers the entropy method and its barrier, the lattice form and structural special cases, average-set-size results, and false strengthenings. Each source carries a read status.
- No rung (μ ≥ 1; an unbounded lower bound; ⌈k/2⌉) is a published theorem.
- The one unconditional margin bound found for separating families is μ ≥ 2k − n, implicit in the published proof of the "at most 2k sets" theorem. [OBSERVED: no violation on all valid families at k = 3, 4, 5.]
- **Still unread:** Poonen (1992) and Abe (2002). The hosts math.mit.edu, sciencedirect.com, doi.org and link.springer.com are refused by the environment. Hu (Discrete Math. 346, 2023) was also not opened.

## 3. Section C — the all-co-singleton sector (`results/proof_sector_full.txt`)

- **C1 [PROVED].** Σ_e g(e) = k(k−2) − N(G), N(G) = Σ (k − 2|A|) over members of size 2..k−2.
- **D₅ [PROVED-EXH].** At k = 5 the sum method gives μ ≥ 3, the true minimum (118,314 sector families).
- **D₆ [PROVED-EXH].** At k = 6 it gives μ ≥ 3; the true minimum is 4 (`mu_search2 6 3 6 1`: 0 found, 7,051,581 nodes; cap 4: 452 families in 14 classes).
- **[PROVED-EXH, k = 3..7]** The minimum of μ over the sector is k − 2.
- **C4 [PROVED].** Frankl's conjecture ⇔ μ ≥ 0 on all valid families containing all co-singletons. Partly known ingredients.
- **C5 [PROVED].** For k ≥ 11 and every top layer T there is a sector family with top layer T and Σ_e g(e) < 0. The sum method is vacuous there.
- **C6 [CONJECTURE].** μ ≥ k − 2 on the sector for all k. Implies Frankl's conjecture. [OBSERVED] no counterexample in a randomised search at k = 8, 9, 10 (`results/proof_sector_break.txt`).

## 4. Section F — structure (`results/proof_extensions.txt`, `results/proof_structure.txt`)

- **Theorem F1 [PROVED].** For t ≥ 2, every valid family on 2t + 1 points with a one-point deletion equal to M₂ₜ has μ ≥ t + 1. [PROVED-EXH] for t = 2, 3, 4: 58, 1,272 and 123,126 extensions, minimum μ = 3, 4, 5.
- **[PROVED-EXH]** The seven minimiser classes at k = 7 are exactly the extensions of M₆ with μ = 4.
- **Proposition F2 [PROVED].** Families I(t,j), 0 ≤ j ≤ t, and II(t) have μ = t + 1 at k = 2t + 1. With B(2,…,2,3) and H_t this is t + 4 families. The four unnamed classes of the 2026-11-09 ledger are I(3,1), I(3,2), I(3,3), II(3) (classes 0, 1, 3, 2).
- **F3 [CONJECTURE]; [PROVED-EXH] t = 3, 4.** These t + 4 families are all the extremal extensions for t ≥ 3. At t = 2 there are 8 classes.

## 5. Section D — Poonen's conjecture on valid families (`results/proof_poonen.txt`)

- **Rung 1 is not proved.**
- **D1 [PROVED].** A smallest valid family with μ ≤ 0 is a core.
- **D2, D3 [PROVED, conditional on Frankl's conjecture on k points; unconditional for k ≤ 12].** μ ≥ 1 if n is odd; μ ≥ j − 2 with j the number of join-irreducible co-singletons; a μ = 0 family is a core with n even, at least two elements at exactly half, and j ≤ 2.
- [OBSERVED, exact count at k = 5] Frankl's conjecture with deletion and parity decides rung 1 for 164,534 of 235,749 valid families (69.8%).
- **D4 [PROVED given Frankl's conjecture on k − 1 points; unconditional for k ≤ 13].** 3μ ≥ 4k − 4 − n. Attained by M₆ and by six of the seven k = 7 minimiser classes.
- **D5 [PROVED].** For k ≤ 13, μ ≥ 1 for every valid family with n ≤ 4k − 5. At k = 8: a valid family with μ ≤ 3 has n = 19 or n ≥ 21.

## 6. Section E — growth (`results/proof_growth.txt`)

- **Rungs 2 and 3 are not proved.**
- **E1, E2 [PROVED, unconditional, every k].** (1 + ψ)μ ≥ 2k − 2 − (1 − ψ)n with ψ = (3 − √5)/2. So μ ≥ 1 whenever n < (1 + √5)(k − 1), and μ grows linearly on slices n ≤ ck, c < 1 + √5.
- [OBSERVED] No valid family with μ < ⌈k/2⌉ in a randomised search at k = 8, 9, 10, 11 (`results/proof_rung3_break.txt`).

## 7. Falsified

- **(i)** "Every minimiser is hub, block or matching type": 6 of 8 classes at k = 4, 41 of 51 at k = 5 and 4 of 7 at k = 7 are none of these.
- **(ii)** one-point deletion to a minimiser: 4/8, 39/51, 0/1, 7/7 (reproduces the 2026-11-09 ledger).
- **W1** "a one- or two-point deletion is a minimiser": 9 of 51 classes at k = 5 have none.
- **W3** "a valid deletion lowers μ by at least 1": 4,570 pairs (G, x) at k = 5 with equality.
- **W2** "a valid deletion never raises μ": true for all valid families at k = 4, 5 [OBSERVED]; false at k = 6: G = {0, 6, 14, 24, 30, 38, 46, 49, 55, 57, 59, 61, 62, 63} has μ = 4 and μ(G − 5) = 5.
- The sum method does not prove μ ≥ 1 on the sector at k = 7: the bound 5 + 3t − α(T) is ≤ 0 for two of the 1,044 top-layer representatives.
